01 / A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Curious by nature.
Analytical by training.

I'm pursuing a B.Tech in Data Science at MPSTME, SVKM's NMIMS, Mumbai. I enjoy working at the intersection of analytical thinking, creative problem-solving, and practical technology.

From cleaning and understanding data to training models and building applications around them, I like exploring the whole process — not just the final accuracy score. I'm especially interested in research that connects AI with biomedical signals and other meaningful real-world challenges.

My goal is to build systems that are useful, carefully evaluated, and grounded in a clear understanding of the problem they're trying to solve.
